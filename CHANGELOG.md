# Changelog

## v1.0a (alpha), 2026-11-19

First public alpha.

- Areas locked behind Unlock items, with the Garage as the hub. Skating into a locked area sends you back to the Garage.
- Checks: 40 level Graffiti Souls, 65 Street Challenge souls, 13 Mystery Tapes, 3 rival gang battles, 5 recruits
  (17 with the Test Run characters), 39 Jet ranks, and 573 graffiti tag spots (optional).
- Items: area Unlocks, characters, Mystery Tape items, Graffiti Soul items, Gouji's Tower Unlock, Spray Can and Health
  refills.
- Goals: Defeat Gouji (Tower requirements: Graffiti Soul items, and optionally tag spots and Street Challenges) or
  Jet Ranks (1-39).
- Corn explains the starting area on a New Game and asks before Gouji's Tower; in a Jet Ranks seed he tracks your Jets.
- Item notices on the game screen in the game's own font. They stay with the game window: a window placed over the game
  covers them, and they hide while the game is minimized.
- The pause screen's Graffiti Soul total shows the Graffiti Soul items you've received.
- The client warns when a seed was made with a different release.
- After a game over sends you to the main menu, a New Game works as usual (Corn's talk leads to the free-skate Garage),
  and the client puts back the progress you made in this seed's game that the save lost: Graffiti Souls, Street
  Challenges, Mystery Tapes, rival gang wins, Jet ranks and graffiti tags (the tags of the area you are in come back once
  you leave it). Restored tags stay sprayed when you skate into their area, where the game would otherwise wipe some of
  them the first time it loads after a New Game. Checks marked done by another world's collect or by a release are never
  put back, so they can't count toward Gouji's Tower or the Jet Ranks goal.
- The Future Site of Rokkaku Expo Stadium: Jazz's recruit check now carries its name ("The Future Site of Rokkaku Expo
  Stadium - Jazz Joins"), and the stadium's save point tag is a new tag check. Both need the 99th Street Unlock. New
  seeds are needed for the new check.
- Picking up a Mystery Tape can no longer leave you stuck in the pickup animation. The game now records the pickup
  itself and removes the tape, even when the client isn't watching at that moment.
